FAQ

What people will ask first

Is Stone Wing unlimited free AI?

No. Stone Wing can use supported provider free tiers where available, but each provider controls its own limits and pricing. The goal is to avoid forcing a separate Stone Wing inference bill on top.

Does Stone Wing replace ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

It can reduce the need to maintain separate interfaces and subscriptions for some workflows, but it does not grant paid entitlements from those services. You connect the providers and access you choose.

Can it work with more than one computer?

Yes, the verified target capability is to connect authorized Mac and Windows computers through Bridge, with governance determining what remote actions are permitted.

What is MCP?

MCP is a standard way for AI systems to connect to compatible tools and services. Stone Wing uses it as part of a broader governed tool layer.

Why governance?

Because an AI that can act on files, tools and computers needs stronger controls than an ordinary chatbot. Stone Wing is designed to make approval and authority boundaries visible.
