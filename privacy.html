Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

This policy describes personal information Spindle & Stone may collect through the Stone Wing website, release-update list, purchase process and support interactions. Third-party AI providers and services you choose to connect have their own privacy practices.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive your name, email address, purchase and billing information supplied through our payment provider, release-list subscription information, support communications, technical diagnostics you choose to provide, and basic website or campaign information supplied by service providers.

2. Release updates

Stone Wing release-update signup is provided through beehiiv. If you subscribe, your email address and related subscription data are processed by that service. You can unsubscribe using the link included in marketing emails.

3. Payments

Stone Wing checkout is processed by Stripe. Payment-card information is handled by Stripe rather than stored directly by the Stone Wing website. Stripe may process identity, payment, fraud-prevention and transaction information under its own terms and privacy practices.

4. Connected AI providers and tools

When you send data to a third-party AI provider, MCP service, API or other connected service through Stone Wing, that data is subject to the provider's terms, privacy practices and your configuration. Stone Wing does not control an independent third party's handling of information.

5. Why information is used

Information may be used to provide and support Stone Wing, administer purchases and subscriptions, send requested communications, maintain security, diagnose problems, meet legal obligations and improve the product and website.

6. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate payments, release communications, hosting, customer support, security or product infrastructure, and when disclosure is required by law. Spindle & Stone does not sell personal information as a standalone product.

7. Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security and legitimate business records.

8. Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. You may request access to or correction of personal information where applicable law provides that right. Purchase, support and privacy-contact instructions are provided on the Stone Wing Support page.

9. Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect personal information, but no online or connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send passwords, complete payment-card numbers or API secrets in support requests.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as Stone Wing, its service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when this policy changes materially.

11. Contact

For privacy, purchase or support requests, follow the customer-contact route on support.html. For billing or transaction-specific requests, keep your Stripe confirmation and include the checkout email so the transaction can be identified.
