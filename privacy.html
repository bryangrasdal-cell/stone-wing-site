Stone Wing Privacy Policy

Draft for launch • September 30, 2026

This policy describes the personal information Spindle & Stone may collect through the Stone Wing website, release list, purchase process, and support interactions.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect your email address, purchase and billing information supplied through our payment provider, support communications, technical diagnostics you choose to provide, and basic website or campaign analytics supplied by service providers.

2. Release-list emails

The release signup is currently provided through beehiiv. If you join the list, your email address and related subscription data are processed through that service. We use the information to send release announcements and related Stone Wing communications. You can unsubscribe using the link included in marketing emails.

3. Payments

Payments will be processed by the payment provider identified at checkout. We do not intend to store complete payment-card numbers on the Stone Wing website.

4. Connected AI providers

When using the Stone Wing application, data sent to a third-party AI provider or connected service is subject to that provider’s privacy terms and your configuration. Stone Wing does not control a third party’s independent handling of information.

5. Why we use information

We use personal information to provide and support Stone Wing, administer purchases and subscriptions, send requested communications, maintain security, diagnose problems, meet legal obligations, and improve the product.

6. Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, newsletter, payments, hosting, customer support, security, or product infrastructure, and when required by law. We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

7. Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security, and legitimate business records.

8. Your choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. You may also request access to or correction of personal information where applicable law provides that right.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect personal information, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the product or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated when required by law.

11. Contact

Before paid sales begin, this page will identify a dedicated privacy/support contact and supplier contact information.
