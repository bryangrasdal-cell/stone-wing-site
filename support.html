SUPPORT

Stone Wing support

Help with purchase records, trial and subscription questions, installation, provider setup, Bridge and governed actions.

Purchase, billing, cancellation or refund

Use the seller contact information included with your Stripe checkout confirmation or receipt so your request can be matched to the transaction. Include the email address used at checkout and the platform purchased. Do not post payment-card information.

Refund & cancellation policy

Installer or access issue

Keep your Stripe confirmation. Stone Wing installer fulfillment is controlled rather than exposed as a public download. If access does not arrive as expected, use the seller contact information in the purchase confirmation and identify whether you purchased Mac or Windows.

Provider or model setup

Stone Wing connects to supported third-party AI providers using accounts or credentials you control. Provider availability, pricing, usage limits and free tiers are controlled by those providers and can change independently.

Bridge and computer connections

Bridge extends Stone Wing to computers you authorize. Pair only computers and environments you control, review the requested action, and use the app’s approval boundaries for consequential work.

What to include in a support request

  • Mac or Windows
  • Purchase or trial email address
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Exact error text when available
  • Relevant screenshots with secrets and API keys removed

Stone Wing does not publish a general support email address on this page because no branded public support mailbox is currently configured. Purchase confirmations remain the authoritative customer-contact path until a branded support address is established.
