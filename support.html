Provider, model or Bridge setup

Stone Wing connects to supported third-party AI providers using accounts or credentials you control. Bridge extends the workspace to computers you authorize. Provider availability, pricing, usage limits and free tiers remain controlled by those providers.

What to include in a support request

  • Mac version and macOS version
  • Purchase or trial email address, if applicable
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Exact error text when available
  • Relevant screenshots with secrets and API keys removed

For transaction-specific help, the Stripe checkout confirmation or receipt remains the authoritative purchase record and includes the seller contact path associated with that transaction.
