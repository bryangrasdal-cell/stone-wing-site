Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

1. Free trials

Stone Wing monthly subscriptions currently begin with a 7-day free trial and require a payment method. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid the first $9.99 CAD monthly charge.

2. Monthly subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled at any time. Cancellation prevents future renewal charges after the applicable paid period. Refunds required by applicable law will be provided within the legally required period.

3. One-time purchases

The $99 CAD one-time purchase is a software license rather than a recurring subscription. Any voluntary refund right specifically offered at checkout or in purchase communications applies in addition to rights required by law. Nothing in this policy limits a refund, cancellation or other remedy that applicable law requires because required information was missing, the product was materially misrepresented or another statutory right applies.

4. Service or price changes

If a subscription change triggers a statutory right to notice, cancellation or another remedy, that right applies regardless of conflicting general language elsewhere on the site.

5. Third-party charges

Charges from AI providers, API vendors, app stores, banks or other third parties are governed by those providers and are not refundable by Spindle & Stone unless Spindle & Stone collected the charge and applicable law requires a refund.

6. How to cancel or request a refund

Subscription customers can use the secure Stone Wing Customer Portal to cancel future renewals, update payment methods and view invoice history. Cancellation through the portal is scheduled for the end of the current billing period.

For refund requests or other transaction-specific help, use the seller contact information included with your Stripe checkout confirmation or receipt so the request can be matched to the correct purchase. Include the checkout email and platform. Do not send complete payment-card numbers or API keys. Additional guidance is available on the Support page.

7. Mandatory rights

This policy does not waive or reduce consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded or modified.
